SOCIOVIA

Bar games for people who haven't met yet.

  1. Get on the bar's WiFi. That's the entire install — no account, no app store, nothing to download.
  2. Join a table, or start one and read the four letters out loud to the people you want to play.
  3. Pick a colour and a symbol so you can actually find each other across a dark, loud room.
  4. Play something daft. The game is the excuse. Talking to strangers is the point.
